Made by Thibaud

Elliniká is a project I made myself, for myself. I wanted to learn Greek, so I built the app I was missing: with sound, games, short stories and a lot of repetition.

A personal project, for now

This app is first of all for my own use and my own goal: learning Greek. It is only the beginning.

Maybe for everyone

Later, I may develop it further, so that it can help everyone who wants to learn Greek.

Privacy

  • No account, no ads, no tracking and no statistics.
  • Your progress, your starred words and your settings stay in your browser, on this device. They are never sent anywhere. If you clear the data of your browser, they are erased: use “Save my progress” to keep a copy.
  • When you are online, the page asks Google Fonts for its font (Google then sees your address, like any website you visit).
  • If you write to me by e-mail, I receive your address and your message, and nothing else.
  • If you sign in with Google, a copy of your progress is kept in a hidden folder of your own Google Drive. It is the only thing the app stores there.
Still in development

Elliniká is still being built. New words, games and fixes are added regularly, so things can change, and some parts may not be perfect yet.

Are you a native Greek speaker?

Your help would be very welcome to develop this app. The review sheet gathers all the Greek of the app: if you speak Greek natively, you can complete it by confirming or correcting each line. The sheet is available in English, French and Greek.
